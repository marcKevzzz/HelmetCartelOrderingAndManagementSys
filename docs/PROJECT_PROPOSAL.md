# Helmet Cartel Ordering and Management System

## Description of the Proposed System

The Helmet Cartel Ordering and Management System is a web-based platform that centralizes helmet and riding-gear inventory tracking and introduces an online storefront for the business. The system replaces the current spreadsheet-based stock tracking method with a real-time inventory database directly linked to sales transactions, ensuring stock counts remain accurate whether a sale occurs online or in-store. Customers are given a public storefront to browse and purchase helmets, while the system is provided a centralized dashboard to manage product listings, process orders, and monitor stock levels reducing reliance on manual record-keeping and minimizing errors caused by staff having to split attention between store operations and inventory upkeep.

## Objectives:

### General Objectives:

This study aims to design and develop a web-based Merchandise and Inventory Management System for Helmet Cartel that replaces manual spreadsheet-based stock tracking with a real-time inventory system and provides an online channel for helmet and merchandise sales.

### Specific Objectives:

- To develop a centralized inventory management module that automatically updates merchandise stock levels with every completed online and in-store sale, eliminating manual reconciliation errors
- To build a public-facing online storefront that allows customers to browse, select, and purchase Helmet Cartel helmets and merchandise without requiring a physical visit
- To provide staff with a dashboard for managing product listings, processing customer orders, and generating sales and inventory reports to support restocking decisions
- To provide registered customers with a self-service account portal to track real-time order fulfillment progress, view digital transaction receipts, and review past payment history

## Scope:

- Merchandise inventory management, stock levels, and low-stock alerts
- Public-facing online storefront for browsing and purchasing helmets
- Order processing with promotional voucher/discount code application and integrated payment options
- Customer account management module featuring profile details, order tracking, and payment transaction history
- Staff dashboard for order fulfillment and status updates
- Basic sales and inventory reporting for restocking decisions
- Intended users: Helmet Cartel staff/admin (inventory and order management) and customers (browsing and purchasing helmets/merchandise online)

## Delimitation:

The system does not cover point-of-sale operations for services outside of merchandise (e.g., helmet fitting consultations or repair services) — it is limited to branded helmets and merchandise only. It does not include third-party courier/shipping logistics beyond basic order status tracking, accounting/bookkeeping functions beyond sales and inventory reports, or customer relationship management (CRM) features such as loyalty programs or marketing automation.

## Core Features:

- **Online Merch Storefront** — public-facing catalog where customers can browse, view details, and purchase Helmet Cartel helmets and accessories online
- **Real-Time Inventory Tracking** — stock levels automatically update with every online and in-store sale, replacing manual spreadsheet counts
- **Order & Fulfillment Management** — staff dashboard to view, process, and update the status of incoming online orders (pending, ready for pickup, completed)
- **Promotions & Voucher Management** — support for applying promotional discount and voucher codes during checkout with automated subtotal recalculation
- **Customer Account & Purchase History** — secure customer portal allowing buyers to track live order statuses, access digital payment receipts (with gateway transaction references), and review previous purchase records
- **Low-Stock Alerts & Reporting** — automatic notifications when merchandise runs low, plus sales/inventory reports to help staff plan restocks
